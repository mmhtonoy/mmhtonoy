3D Milky Way Galaxy

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy that contains our Solar System. It spans over 100,000 light-years in diameter and harbors between 100 to 400 billion stars, including our Sun.

At its supermassive core lies Sagittarius A*, a massive black hole holding together our galactic neighborhood in an intricate spiral dance.
